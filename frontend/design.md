# Design System (Blue & White + Tailwind)

## Palette
- **Background**: `bg-white` (#FFFFFF) or `bg-blue-50` (Very light blue background)
- **Primary**: `bg-blue-600` (#2563EB)
- **Foreground**: `text-blue-950` (#172554)
- **Borders**: `border-blue-200` (#BFDBFE)
- **Hover**: `hover:bg-blue-700 hover:text-white`

## Components

### Buttons
- **Primary (Solid)**: `h-10 px-4 border border-blue-600 bg-blue-600 text-white rounded text-sm font-medium hover:bg-blue-700 transition-colors`
- **Secondary (Minimal Icon)**: `bg-transparent text-slate-400 hover:text-red-500 hover:bg-slate-100 p-2 rounded transition-all duration-200`

### Typography
- **H1**: `text-2xl font-bold text-blue-950`
- **H2**: `text-xl font-bold text-blue-950`
- **H3**: `text-base font-semibold text-blue-950`
- **Body**: `text-sm font-normal text-blue-800`

## Spacing & Layout
- **Component Wrapper Cards**: `bg-white rounded border border-blue-200 p-6 shadow-sm`
- **Form Rows (Vertical Stacking)**: `flex flex-col gap-4`
- **Label-to-Input Gap**: `flex flex-col gap-1.5`
- **Dashboard Grid**: `grid grid-cols-1 md:grid-cols-3 gap-6`
